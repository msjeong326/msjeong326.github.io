About

What I Do

I study how environmental conditions reshape plant-associated microbial communities and their contribution to plant health. I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Melotto Lab at UC Davis, combining gnotobiotic experiments, multi-omics, and computational ecology with research on crop and aquatic microbiomes.
